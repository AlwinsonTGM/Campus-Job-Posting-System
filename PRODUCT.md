# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

KLD students find and apply for vacancies. Verified employers manage vacancies and applications, and administrators oversee the system.

## Product Purpose

Campus Hire connects students with suitable opportunities and supports the hiring process from vacancy posting through application review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Students can review openings, declare free shifts, and track applications.
- Employers can post vacancies and evaluate applicants.
- Administrators oversee verification and the system.
- The website supports light and dark themes.
- Keep text readable on different screen sizes, show available openings clearly, and make evaluation stages move forward.

## Brand Commitments

The existing product name is Campus Hire.
